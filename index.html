Hsin-Ying Lee

Building AI-native tools for designing the physical world.

CTO at illoca, where we are building Plamo—an AI-native 3D modeling tool that turns sketches, photos, and text into fully editable parametric models—and Tracing Paper for architectural design.

My work bridges generative AI, 3D/4D visual computing, and tools for spatial design. Previously, I led the 3D/4D generation team on the Creative Vision team at Snap Research. I received my PhD from UC Merced, advised by Prof. Ming-Hsuan Yang.

Architectural design

Tracing Paper

illoca is creating an intelligent canvas for architectural design: a way for architects to stay close to sketches, markups, references, and language while producing editable 2D and 3D design data.

The product direction is simple: keep the creative act lightweight, make the AI outputs editable, and let designers move fluidly between early concepts and structured architectural representations.
